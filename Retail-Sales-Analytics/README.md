# 📌 Project Overview

Retail organizations generate thousands of sales transactions every day. This project analyzes retail sales data to uncover valuable business insights, monitor key performance indicators (KPIs), identify sales opportunities, and support strategic business decisions.

The project follows a complete AI Business Analyst lifecycle, including business documentation, SQL analysis, Power BI dashboard development, and AI-based business recommendations.

---

# 💼 Business Problem

RetailMart experiences inconsistent sales performance across stores, regions, and product categories. Management lacks a centralized reporting solution to answer important business questions such as:

- Which regions generate the highest revenue?
- Which products generate the highest profit?
- Which customer segments contribute the most sales?
- Which categories underperform?
- How can profitability be improved?

---

# 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze retail sales performance
- Monitor executive KPIs
- Identify top-performing products
- Improve profitability
- Understand customer purchasing behavior
- Analyze regional sales performance
- Build an executive Power BI dashboard
- Provide AI-driven business recommendations
